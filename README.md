## About the Game

*Mourneveil* is an independent incremental horror game set in the ruins of an ancient vampire dynasty.

After centuries of slumber, a vampire heir awakens to find their once-powerful family nearly forgotten. The castle stands in decay, its servants are gone, and its halls conceal a history that refuses to stay buried.

Restore the estate, gather blood, reclaim forbidden knowledge, and rebuild the dynasty. But every step toward power stirs something within the castle itself.

**The stronger the dynasty becomes, the more the castle awakens.**

## Planned Gameplay

- **Incremental progression:** Generate resources, purchase upgrades, and build increasingly efficient production systems.
- **Castle restoration:** Reopen chambers that introduce new mechanics and opportunities.
- **Automation:** Recruit servants and unlock ways to expand production beyond manual actions.
- **Awakening system:** Growing power affects the castle, introducing unsettling changes and supernatural events.
- **Mystery and narrative:** Recover fragments of the Mourneveil family's past through exploration and progression.
- **Prestige with a story:** Enter a centuries-long slumber, restart part of your progression, and uncover lasting powers and memories.

*These are design goals, not a list of completed features. Mechanics may change as development progresses.*

## Design Pillars

| Pillar | Our Goal |
| --- | --- |
| **Satisfying progression** | Make each upgrade and new production chain feel meaningful. |
| **Gothic atmosphere** | Build a world of candlelit chambers, ancient portraits, secrets, and vampire aristocracy. |
| **Horror through gameplay** | Let the player's growth directly influence tension and supernatural events. |
| **A manageable scope** | Build a polished, playable core before expanding the castle and its story. |
